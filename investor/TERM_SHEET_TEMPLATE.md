# Series A Preferred Stock Term Sheet Template

**Issuer**: VeriDAG Sovereign Settlement Labs, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "Company")  
**Investors**: Lead Investor and participating syndicate ("Investors")  
**Securities**: Series A Preferred Stock  
**Investment Amount**: $10,000,000 ("Aggregate Investment")  
**Pre-Money Valuation**: $40,000,000 (Post-Money Valuation: $50,000,000)

---

## 1. Investment Terms & Liquidation Preference

1. **Purchase Price**: Calculated by dividing the Pre-Money Valuation by the fully diluted capitalization of the Company immediately prior to closing.
2. **Liquidation Preference**: One times (1.0x) the Original Issue Price plus declared but unpaid dividends on each share of Series A Preferred Stock. Non-participating (Series A holders choose either preference or common share conversion).
3. **Dividends**: Non-cumulative dividends payable when, as, and if declared by the Board of Directors, pari passu with Common Stock.

---

## 2. Voting Rights & Board Representation

1. **General Voting**: Series A Preferred Stock votes together with Common Stock on an as-converted basis on all matters submitted to stockholders.
2. **Board of Directors**: The Board shall consist of five (5) members upon closing:
   - Two (2) designated by Common Stock holders (Founders).
   - One (1) designated by the Lead Series A Investor.
   - One (1) designated by the Consortium Validator Governance Council.
   - One (1) independent industry expert mutually agreed upon by Common and Preferred holders.

---

## 3. Investor Protective Provisions

So long as at least 25% of the Series A Preferred Stock remains outstanding, the Company shall not take any of the following actions without the affirmative vote of the holders of a majority of Series A Preferred Stock:
- Alter or change the rights, preferences, or privileges of the Series A Preferred Stock.
- Create or authorize the issuance of any new class of shares having rights senior to or on parity with the Series A Preferred Stock.
- Increase or decrease the authorized number of shares of Common Stock or Preferred Stock.
- Liquidate, dissolve, or effect any sale, merger, or change of control of the Company.
- Repurchase or redeem any shares of capital stock other than unvested founder shares pursuant to equity incentive plans.

---

## 4. Transfer Restrictions, Preemptive & Information Rights

1. **Right of First Refusal & Co-Sale**: Investors have a right of first refusal and secondary co-sale right with respect to proposed transfers of stock by key founders.
2. **Preemptive (Pro-Rata) Rights**: Major Investors (holding at least $1,000,000 in Series A shares) have the right to participate pro-rata in subsequent equity financing rounds to maintain their ownership percentage.
3. **Information Rights**: Quarterly financial statements (within 45 days of quarter end), annual audited financial statements (within 120 days of fiscal year end), and annual operating budgets.
4. **Registration Rights**: Standard demand, piggyback, and S-3 registration rights beginning five (5) years post-closing or six (6) months following a qualified initial public offering (IPO).

---

## 5. Exclusivity & Expenses

1. **Exclusivity**: For a period of thirty (30) days from signing, the Company agrees not to solicit, encourage, or engage in any discussions regarding alternative financing or acquisition transactions.
2. **Legal Fees**: The Company shall reimburse the Lead Investor for reasonable legal fees and diligence expenses incurred up to a maximum of $50,000 upon closing.

# Letter of Intent (LOI): Institutional Settlement Pilot Partnership
## USMCA Cross-Border Trade & Real-Time Treasury Clearance

**Date**: September 2026  
**Between**:  
1. **VeriDAG Sovereign Settlement Labs, Inc.** ("VeriDAG")  
2. **Pilot Commercial Partner** ("Partner"): [Enterprise Freight Forwarder / Trade Clearinghouse / AI Platform]

---

## 1. Intent of the Parties

The purpose of this non-binding Letter of Intent ("LOI") is to express the mutual interest of VeriDAG and Partner to collaborate on a commercial pilot program deploying VeriDAG's sovereign digital dollar (USDV) and Settler reconciliation engine to clear cross-border commercial trade invoices across the United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement (USMCA) corridor.

---

## 2. Pilot Objectives & Scope

During the pilot program, the parties will:
1. **Pilot Settlement Volume**: Target the clearance of an initial **$5,000,000 to $15,000,000 USD** in commercial freight invoices and vendor payables using USDV.
2. **Settler Proofpack Anchoring**: Integrate Partner's ERP/TMS system with VeriDAG via the TypeScript/Python SDKs to anchor reconciliation proofpacks (`SETTLER_ANCHOR`) directly into consensus state roots.
3. **Latency & Cost Benchmarking**: Measure transaction finality times (targeting $<300\text{ms}$ vs 72-hour SWIFT wires) and fee savings (targeting $\ge 75\%$ reduction in wire and FX fees).
4. **Reserve Transparency**: Provide Partner treasury teams with automated daily Proof-of-Reserves cryptographic verification via the VeriDAG Enterprise portal.

---

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

### A. Responsibilities of VeriDAG
- Provide dedicated solutions engineering support for SDK and API integration.
- Facilitate institutional fiat on/off-ramp onboarding with VeriDAG's Qualified Custodian banking partners.
- Guarantee par liquidity ($1.00 USDV = $1.00 USD) and zero settlement variance.
- Waive protocol transaction fees during the initial 90-day pilot window.

### B. Responsibilities of Partner
- Identify an initial cohort of 3 to 5 vetted commercial shippers/carriers for pilot invoice settlement.
- Integrate VeriDAG SDK into pilot payment dispatch pipelines.
- Provide bi-weekly operational feedback to the VeriDAG engineering team regarding performance, reporting, and ERP reconciliation.

---

## 4. Path to Commercial Master Agreement

Upon the successful conclusion of the 90-day pilot program and satisfaction of agreed success criteria (100% uptime, zero settlement discrepancies, $<500\text{ms}$ settlement latency), the parties intend in good faith to enter into a definitive **Commercial Master Services Agreement** designating VeriDAG as a preferred cross-border settlement rail for Partner's ongoing commercial operations.

---

## 5. Non-Binding Nature & Confidentiality

Except for Section 5 (Confidentiality and Exclusivity), this LOI represents the intentions of the parties only and does not create any legally binding commercial or financial obligation. All proprietary technical and commercial information exchanged shall remain strictly confidential under standard mutual NDA terms.

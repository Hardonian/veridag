## 1. Executive Unit Economics Summary

VeriDAG possesses one of the highest operating margin profiles in modern financial technology because reserve asset management generates continuous risk-free cash flows from the macro interest rate environment while protocol throughput scales with near-zero marginal computational cost:

```
Total Net Revenue = [USDV Float × Treasury Yield] + [Tx Volume × Protocol Fee] - [Custody & Staking Share]
```

At an illustrative 4.50% blended US Treasury Bill yield:
- **$100M USDV Float** = **$4,500,000 Annual Gross Float Yield**
- **$500M USDV Float** = **$22,500,000 Annual Gross Float Yield**
- **$1.0B USDV Float** = **$45,000,000 Annual Gross Float Yield**

## 3. Revenue Share & Incentive Waterfall

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 Gross Treasury Float Yield                  │
└──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                               │
       ┌───────────────────────┼───────────────────────┐
       ▼                       ▼                       ▼
60% Protocol Treasury   30% Consortium Validators  10% Settlement Partners
• Continuous R&D        • Staking Yield Incentives • Volume Rebate Pool
• Security Audits       • Zero-Slashing Bonuses   • High-volume USMCA Importers
• Reserve Buffer Fund   • Hardware Operations      • Liquidity Routing Desks
```

1. **Protocol Treasury (60%)**: Reinvested into protocol core engineering, security verification, emergency liquidity reserves, and corporate profitability.
2. **Consortium Validator Staking (30%)**: Distributed to dynamic committee validators in proportion to their weighted stake, aligning institutional node operators with long-term network security and high uptime.
3. **High-Volume Settlement Partner Rebates (10%)**: Returned as fee credits and commercial rebates to USMCA corporate clearinghouses that commit more than $50M in annual settlement turnover.

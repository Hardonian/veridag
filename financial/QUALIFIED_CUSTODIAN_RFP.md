# Request for Proposal (RFP): Institutional Qualified Custody & Reserve Administration
## Sovereign Digital Dollar (USDV) Reserve Trust

**Issuing Entity**: VeriDAG Sovereign Settlement Labs, Inc.  
**RFP Reference**: RFP-CUSTODY-2026-Q3  
**Target Vendors**: BNY Mellon, State Street, Anchorage Digital Bank, Cantor Fitzgerald  
**Submission Deadline**: October 15, 2026  
**Primary Contact**: Treasury Committee (treasury@veridag.network)

---

## 1. Project Background & Objective

VeriDAG is launching **USDV**, an institutional digital dollar built specifically for sovereign trade clearing across the USMCA economic corridor, cross-chain multi-asset settlement, and autonomous AI agent economies. 

VeriDAG invites tier-1 qualified custodians and chartered trust companies to submit formal proposals to serve as the **Primary Qualified Custodian and Reserve Administrator** for the USDV Reserve Trust.

---

## 2. Scope of Services Required

The selected institution will provide:
1. **Bankruptcy-Remote Fiduciary Custody**: Establishment of segregated trust accounts designated for the benefit of USDV holders under US law.
2. **Treasury Management & Sweep Execution**:
   - Automated primary market execution in 4-week and 13-week US Treasury Bills via direct Fed auction or primary dealer access.
   - Overnight sweep functionality into Federal Reserve Reverse Repurchase Agreements (ON RRP).
   - Demand deposit cash management for intraday fiat wire settlement.
3. **Automated Cryptographic Telemetry (API & Signature)**:
   - Secure REST/WebSocket API providing continuous real-time balance reporting.
   - Capability to submit cryptographic attestation signatures via an institutional HSM for integration into the VeriDAG Proof-of-Reserves state root.
4. **Independent Audit Co-Operation**:
   - Monthly confirmation and reconciliation data provided directly to VeriDAG's independent CPA firm (RSM / Grant Thornton) for AT-C 205 attestation reports.
5. **Direct Banking Rails**:
   - FedNow, RTP, and Fedwire integration for same-day institutional minting and redemption.

---

## 3. Proposal Questionnaire & Technical Evaluation

Responding institutions must address:
- **Charter & Regulatory Oversight**: State or federal banking charter (OCC, NYDFS, Wyoming Division of Banking), FDIC insurance limits, and supervisory status.
- **Balance Sheet & Capitalization**: Tier 1 risk-based capital ratio, assets under custody (AUC), and credit ratings from major rating agencies (Moody's, S&P, Fitch).
- **Insurance Coverage**: Specie and crime insurance coverage limits, cyber insurance limits, and depository liability coverage.
- **Pricing & Fee Schedule**:
   - Custody basis points on average monthly assets under custody (e.g., $10M–$100M, $100M–$500M, >$500M tiers).
   - Fixed account administration and reporting fees.
   - Fedwire, RTP, and FedNow transaction fees.
- **Disaster Recovery & Redundancy**: Recovery Time Objective (RTO) and Recovery Point Objective (RPO) metrics for primary custody and wire facilities.
